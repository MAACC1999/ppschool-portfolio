# PP School

PP School is a parent-facing learning companion designed to help families organise learning without treating curriculum coverage, school exposure, learner self-report, or one practice result as the same thing as mastery.

The project combines product design, an independent real-world knowledge architecture, and a human-controlled knowledge-governance model. Implementation was AI-assisted, but the product direction and core architecture are the owner's independently designed contributions.

## Transferable Data Integrity and Governance

For roles involving data quality, operational records or controlled workflows, PP School demonstrates transferable practices in:

- keeping different evidence types separate instead of collapsing them into one status;
- preserving source traceability and explicit validation rules;
- representing incomplete, conflicting or insufficient evidence without silently upgrading it;
- enforcing controlled status transitions and human approval boundaries; and
- making release or publication authority explicit when automated assistance is involved.

This is a capability-transfer explanation, not a claim of direct warehouse, physical stocktaking, WMS, manufacturing or production quality-system experience.

## Three design contributions

1. **Product vision and learning model:** a parent-facing workflow that separates curriculum requirements, school exposure, learner declarations, and direct practice evidence.
2. **Independent real-world knowledge architecture:** a domain backbone organised through phenomena, concepts, models, methods, evidence, constraints, and applications, with official requirements mapped onto it.
3. **Knowledge governance:** source-grounded review and release controls that preserve challenge, abstention, dissent, deterministic verification, and human authority over formal publication.
